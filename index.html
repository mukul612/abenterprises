1. 01

    Alternating current in the coil

    The IGBT inverter feeds the coil with high-frequency AC, generating a rapidly changing magnetic field around the part.

  2. 02

    Eddy currents in the workpiece

    That field induces circulating eddy currents inside the conductive part — heat is generated inside the metal.

  3. 03

    Targeted heat zone

    Frequency sets the depth of penetration. Coil geometry sets the heat pattern. We tune both for your part.

  4. 04

    Quench & finish

    For hardening, an integrated polymer or water quench locks in martensite. The result: a hardened skin over a tough core.
