02

What we heat

Eight industrial processes covered across medium- and high-frequency bands. Each cell engineered around the part — inductor geometry, dwell, ramp profile and quench timed to metallurgy.

8 industrial processes
  • 01 Hardening Med-frequency · 3 – 30 kHz

    Surface and through-hardening of axles, shafts, gears and rings. Repeatable case depth.

  • 02 Brazing High-frequency · 30 – 500 kHz

    Single- and double-station brazing for tipped tools, copper-to-steel, carbide tooling.

  • 03 Annealing Med-frequency · 3 – 30 kHz

    Localised stress relief and softening — copper, brass, mild steel sub-assemblies.

  • 04 Pre-heating High-frequency · 30 – 500 kHz

    Ramp before welding, forging or forming. Tight thermal window, no scaling.

  • 05 Shrink-fitting Med-frequency · 3 – 30 kHz

    Bearing rings, bushes, hubs onto shafts. Uniform expansion, repeatable seating.

  • 06 Billet heating High-frequency · 30 – 500 kHz

    End and through-heating of billets ahead of forging. PLC-cycled for throughput.

  • 07 End heating Med-frequency · 3 – 30 kHz

    Bar ends for upsetting and bending — fast, localised, repeatable.

  • 08 Bonding High-frequency · 30 – 500 kHz

    Adhesive curing, plastic-to-metal bonding, susceptor heating.

05

Engineering process

From component drawing to factory acceptance test — five steps, six to twelve weeks. Every machine ships only after a verified trial heat with your part.

6 – 12 weeks · drawing to FAT
  1. 01

    Component & metallurgy review

    You send the part drawing, material grade and target spec — case depth, HRC, throughput, quench medium. Our engineering team confirms feasibility within 48 hours.

  2. 02

    Inductor & cycle design

    Custom inductor geometry modelled for your part. Power, frequency and dwell calculated against the metallurgical envelope. Drawings shared for sign-off.

  3. 03

    Build & integration

    Power supply assembled, inductor wound and brazed, PLC programmed, HMI screens authored. Cabinet wired, hi-pot tested, dielectric verified.

  4. 04

    Trial heat with your component

    Sample parts heated on our floor. Coupons sectioned, hardness traversed, case depth measured. Cycle tuned until results sit inside spec with margin.

  5. 05

    Commissioning & training

    Machine packed, freighted and commissioned on your floor. Operators and maintenance trained on standard operation, recipe management and inductor change-over.
