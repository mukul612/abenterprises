Tale of the tape

Built in the cage. Forged in the 313.

Jesse started his amateur MMA career in 2010, turned professional in 2015, and tested himself in high-level promotions including Bellator MMA. He's lived every part of the sport — the weight cuts, the fight-week nerves, the walkouts and the wars.

In 2022 he opened Bloom MMA & Fitness to give Allen Park what he wished he had on day one: pro-level coaching, a family-first room, and a corner that never quits on you — whether you're training for health, local competition, or a professional career.
