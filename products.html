03

Options & automation

Standard machines accept a wide range of automation, scanning and quench options. Most are hot-swappable on the same power supply.

Engineered to order
  • A1 Vertical scanner Single-shot or scan-quench

    Servo-driven Z-axis with polymer or water spray quench ring. Recipe-driven from HMI.

  • A2 Horizontal scanner Bar / shaft hardening

    Twin-spindle rotation with scanning inductor. Suitable for long shafts and rolls.

  • A3 Dual-station Throughput optimisation

    Operator loads station B while station A heats. Cycle masking doubles throughput on brazing cells.

  • A4 CNC scanning Multi-axis profile

    Programmable profile-tracking for complex geometries — cam lobes, splines, gear teeth.

  • A5 Robotic load / unload 6-axis integration

    Fanuc, ABB or Kuka integration with cycle-side handshake. Tested on customer floors.

  • A6 Quench plant Polymer · water · oil

    Closed-loop quench with concentration, temperature and flow control. Logged to recipe.

  • A7 Pyrometer feedback Optical · two-colour

    Closed-loop temperature control. Cycle terminates on target reach instead of fixed time.

  • A8 Recipe management HMI · barcode · Modbus

    Per-part recipes with role-based access. SCADA logging and Modbus / Ethernet IP integration.
